# What korea-data-mcp handles for you

**English** | [한국어](QUIRKS.ko.md)

Korean public APIs work, but each one has its own surprises. An agent that calls them directly has to discover every one of these by failing, and some of them do not fail at all: they return a plausible but wrong answer.

Everything below was **observed on the live APIs** (last checked 2026-11-11), and the real responses are kept in each provider's `testdata/` so the handling is tested.

Status: ✅ handled · 🚧 handled when the tool that needs it ships

## Wrong answers without an error

These are the most dangerous ones: nothing fails, the agent simply gets the wrong number.

| Source | What the API does | What korea-data-mcp does | |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenDART | In half-year and quarterly reports, income statement items are labeled with the year-to-date range (`2025.01.01 ~ 2025.06.30`) but `thstrm_amount` holds **only the latest quarter**. The year-to-date figure is in a separate `thstrm_add_amount`. | Every amount is returned with the period it covers: `current` is year-to-date, `current_quarter` the latest three months, each with its own `start` and `end`. If a filing ever lacks the year-to-date figure, the amount is marked instead of being labeled year-to-date. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (dividends) | In half-year and quarterly reports the current column is year-to-date, but **the two other columns are full prior years**, not the same months: Samsung's 2025 half-year report puts ₩732 per share next to ₩1,446 for all of 2024. | Each period says whether it is a full year and, for the report's own period, the last day covered. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (dividends) | Per-share figures are not adjusted for stock splits: Samsung paid ₩21,000 a share for 2015 and ₩1,446 for 2024, before and after its 50:1 split. Units are only in the row labels (`(백만원)`, `(원)`, `(%)`). | Amounts in KRW, with the par value of each year so a split is visible. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (major shareholders) | Relations are free text (`본인`, `최대주주`, `최대주주 본인`, `본인(최대주주)`), rows can be `"` (ditto) for the row above, names carry layout spaces (`김   식`), and group totals are rows named `계`. | The largest shareholder is identified, ditto rows resolved, names cleaned, and each holder's common and preferred rows merged; totals are separate. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (major decisions) | The date range selects decisions by the date they were **first filed**, but each row is the **latest correction**, with no date: 광진실업's rights offering filed on 2026-07-02 comes back as receipt `20261029000002` and only for a July range. Asking for October's decisions misses it, and reading the receipt number dates it to October. | `filed_date` is the latest filing, `board_date` the board resolution (when the filing has one), and the description says what the range matches. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (buybacks) | Buyback amounts are **split by share class**: Samsung's ₩3 trillion buyback of November 2024 is ₩2.68 trillion in the common-share field and ₩0.32 trillion in the preferred one. | `amount.total` adds them up, next to each class. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (buybacks) | A buyback through a trust (자기주식취득신탁계약) is a **different report and endpoint**: LG Chem's ₩300 billion trust of 2026-10 has no buyback decision at all, so "did LG Chem announce a buyback?" gets "no". | Trust contracts and their termination are listed with direct buybacks. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (rights offerings) | The decision has **no issue price, no allottees and no board date**, only the new shares and the proceeds by purpose. | `implied_price` is the proceeds divided by the new shares (₩2,865 for 광진실업), and `new_shares_percent` the dilution. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (convertible bonds) | The conversion price can be **reset down to a floor** (리픽싱), reported in a separate field: 파이온엑스's ₩557 bond can convert at ₩21, its par value of USD 0.015, which turns 4.3 million shares into 114 million. | `floor_price` and `shares_at_floor` next to the price and shares. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (5% holders) | The 5% holdings API returns **only about the last two years** of reports. The National Pension Service last reported on Samsung Electronics in 2022 and on SK hynix in 2024-09, so both are missing, and "who holds 5% or more?" gets only the controlling group. | The disclosure list, which goes back to 2015, is checked too: reporters missing from the API are listed in `older_reporters` with their last filing. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (5% holders) | The reports are a history, not a list of holders: a holder that sold down to 3.53% or filed **0.00% because its representative reporter changed** (The Capital Group → Capital Research on SK hynix) still appears with its earlier reports. | `holders` has each reporter's latest stake with a status: `holding`, `below_5_percent` or `exited`. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (insiders) | The main shareholder field is as filed: Samsung executives holding 561 to 1,742 shares are marked **10%이상주주** (10% or more holder). Most of the 3,368 reports in two years are stock grants to executives. | The field is passed on as `main_shareholder`, described as unchecked, next to the actual `percent`; reports can be filtered by reporter, date and direction. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (investments) | A report whose table was filed only as an attachment comes back as **success with a row of `-` and a `합계` row**: Samsung Electronics and SK hynix for 2015 to 2020. It reads as "no stakes in other companies". | No items, and a note saying the table is in the filing's attachment, with its url. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (investments) | A stake **sold entirely during the year** has `-` at the end of the period, the same `-` that elsewhere means a written-off book value of a stake still held: SK Square's 80.3% of 11번가 and its stakes in 코빗 and 스파크플러스 look like missing values. | `exited: true`, listed after the stakes still held, and counted in `summary.exited`. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (investments) | Amounts have **no currency**. Tables in 천원 or 백만원 are converted to KRW, but 소마젠, a US company listed on KOSDAQ, files in USD with only a footnote saying so: its 156,006 dollar stake in Macrogen Japan comes back as ₩156,006. | A note on every response for a foreign company listed in Korea. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (investments) | The investee's total assets and net income can be in **another currency, noted only in the purpose cell**: 유성기업's `일반투자\n(연결총자산,순이익\n단위:백만엔)` for NITTAN VALVE, whose ¥66.6 billion of assets reads as ₩66.6 billion. | The note is moved to `investee.unit_note`, and the purpose is cleaned. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (full statements) | **83 of 90 statements have no income statement (IS)**: revenue and operating income are in the comprehensive income statement (CIS), so reading IS finds no revenue. Companies that file both repeat 당기순이익. | Both are one `income_statement`, with 당기순이익 once. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (full statements) | Cash flow lines are XBRL facts, not the printed signs: **money paid out is positive**. Samsung's dividends (₩9.9 trillion), capex (₩47.5 trillion) and buybacks (₩8.2 trillion) are positive while the financing total is -₩13.5 trillion, so adding up lines gives a wrong total. | Standard outflow lines are marked `flow: outflow`, and the response says how signs work. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (full statements) | In half and quarterly reports `thstrm_amount` is the **latest quarter for income items but year-to-date for cash flows**, and a cash flow's `frmtrm_q_amount` ("quarter") is the prior year-to-date: SK hynix's ₩91.7 trillion operating cash flow is January to June. | Every amount comes with its start and end dates. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (fiscal year) | For a fiscal year not ending in December, **`bsns_year` names different years in annual and interim reports**: for 동원모빌리티 (March year-end) the 2025 annual report is April 2024 to March 2025, but the 2025 first quarter is April to June 2025, and a 2026 half-year report does not exist yet. | Periods are dated from the fiscal year-end month, and the tool description and response explain the rule. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (full statements) | Banks, insurers and securities firms have **no revenue line** (6 of 90 statements): KB Financial reports 보험수익, 보험서비스결과 and 금융상품순손익. "What was its revenue?" finds nothing or the wrong line. | A note says the company has no revenue line and where its income is. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr | An invalid parameter is not an error. `DEAL_YMD=2025` (instead of `202509`) or `solMonth=13` returns **success with zero results**, which looks like "no data". | Every tool validates its inputs and returns `INVALID_ARGUMENT` with the expected format. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (holidays) | Years without data (before 2004, or more than two years ahead) also return **success with zero results**, so "no holidays in 2030" looks like a fact. | The year range is checked, and the error message states it. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (holidays) | The "national holidays" (국경일) operation returns **all 22 public holidays** of 2026, the same as the "public holidays" operation, not the five national holidays. | That operation is not exposed. Public holidays come from the public holidays operation only. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (apartment trades) | Prices are strings in **units of 10,000 KRW** (`"84,500"` is ₩845,000,000). | Amounts are plain KRW integers. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (apartment trades) | **Cancelled deals stay in the data**, marked only by `cdealType: "O"`: 12 of 233 Gangnam trades in 2025-09, including a ₩7.5 billion deal re-signed the same day for ₩7.25 billion. Counting them skews prices. | Excluded by default and never counted in the price summary; `include_cancelled` lists them with the cancellation date. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (apartment trades) | When a city is reorganized, **past data moves to the new district codes**. 화성시's well-known code `41590` now returns zero trades even for 2021; the data is under its four new 구 codes. | A district table generated from the official code list. Districts are given by name, and a city name expands to all its districts: `화성시` (or `41590`) queries 41591, 41593, 41595 and 41597. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (apartment trades) | 광주광역시 (`29xxx`) and 전라남도 (`46xxx`) merged into 전남광주통합특별시 (`12xxx`), and 인천's 중구, 동구 and 서구 became 제물포구, 영종구, 서해구 and 검단구. The old codes return zero trades, even for 2025. | `광주 북구`, `광주광역시 광산구` and `전남 목포시` resolve to the new codes. An old code or name is an error that names its replacement, not an empty result. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (apartment rents) | **Jeonse and monthly rent are one list**, told apart only by `monthlyRent` `"0"`, and **renewals are mixed with new contracts**: 97 of 217 Jongno contracts in 2025-09. A renewal under the tenant's right (계약갱신요구권) is capped at +5%, so the mixed median monthly rent was ₩590,000 against ₩960,000 for new contracts alone. | Separate jeonse and monthly summaries with new and renewal counts; `contract: new` gives market prices. Each contract shows its term and, for a renewal, the previous deposit and rent. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (apartment rents) | New and renewal are reported only since June 2021, and not for every contract after that (17 of the 217 above). | Contracts of unknown type are counted only when `contract` is not set. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (apartment trades) | Deals are reported up to 30 days after the contract, and cancellations months later, so the latest month looks quiet (4 trades in Jongno for 2026-10 as of the 25th). | Stated in the tool description so the agent does not read it as a market drop. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (company info) | The registry keeps **history**: Samsung Electronics has 19 records, returned in no particular order. Older ones name a former CEO, partial ones have no market and a salary of 0. Taking the first record can give years-old data. | Only the latest record is used, and `as_of` shows its date (some companies were last updated in 2020). | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (company info) | Unknown employee counts and salaries are `0`, not empty. | `0` is treated as unknown and omitted, never shown as "0 employees". | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (stock prices) | Querying by name (`itmsNm`) matches the name **on each day**, so a renamed company's history is cut at the rename: `POSCO홀딩스` returns 1,127 of 1,671 days. | Prices are always queried by code; days before a rename carry their old `name`. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (stock prices) | The exact-code parameter `srtnCd` is **silently ignored** and returns every stock (4.5 million rows). `likeSrtnCd` is a substring match (`00593` also matches 삼성전자우). | A full 6-character code with `likeSrtnCd`, which is then exact. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (stock prices) | Name search is case- and space-sensitive: `naver` and `삼성 전자` return nothing. | Names are matched against the latest day's listing, ignoring case and spaces; an ambiguous name lists the candidates with codes. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (stock prices) | On a day without trades (e.g. a trading halt), open, high and low are **0** while the close is the last price, so "low: ₩0" appears. | `no_trades: true`, and open/high/low are omitted. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (stock prices) | `endBasDt` is exclusive and an invalid date (`2026-10-23` instead of `20261023`) is success with zero results. A preferred share's ISIN does not contain its code (삼성전자우: `KR7005931001`, code `005935`). | `from`/`to` are inclusive ISO dates and validated; ISINs are looked up, not sliced. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (business status) | A business number written with hyphens (`124-81-00998`, the usual format) is reported as **not registered with the NTS**, even for Samsung Electronics. | Hyphens and spaces are removed before the lookup; anything that is not 10 digits is rejected with the offending value. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (pension workplaces) | Every month is a **separate record**, and a search returns the last 12 months mixed together: Samsung Electronics' head office appears 12 times among 58 records. The month cannot be filtered. | Records are folded into one workplace each, described by its latest month; the other months become an optional `history`. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (pension workplaces) | The business registration number is published as its **first 6 digits** (`124810****`), shared with other companies (601 workplaces under `124810`). The full number or a hyphenated one is success with zero results. | `business_number` takes 10 digits, hyphens or 6 digits and sends the first 6, together with the name. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (pension workplaces) | Workplace name search is a raw substring match: `삼성전자` returns 1,739 records, mostly cafés, daycare centers and contractors' sites at Samsung plants, while `삼성전자(주)` with the number prefix, `삼성 전자` and `NAVER` (filed as `네이버 주식회사`) return nothing. Hyundai Motor's head office is not among the first 300 records for `현대자동차`. | Corporate forms and spaces are stripped, the exact name comes first, and the description tells the agent to pass the business number from opendart or datagokr. A name that matches more than 1,000 records says so. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (pension workplaces) | A company files short-term sites as **separate workplaces** under its own name (`삼성전자(주)/일용/…냉난방기 구매설치`: 46 for Samsung, most withdrawn). Added up, they would distort the headcount. | Each workplace has `kind`: `workplace`, or `site` when 일용 or 상용 is set off as a label; enrolled company workplaces come first. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (pension workplaces) | The data trails by about two months, and the billed amount moves with policy, not pay: Hyundai Motor's jumped 4.6% in 2026-01 (the contribution rate went from 9% to 9.5%) and 7% in 2026-07 (the income ceiling's yearly update) while subscribers fell. Dividing it by subscribers is not a salary. | `as_of` and each record's `month` are shown; the note says the amount is capped and is not a salary. | ✅ |
| OpenDART | Net income appears twice with identical values (once in the income statement, once at the top of comprehensive income). | Exact duplicates are removed. | ✅ |
| law.go.kr | The "current law" text (`target=law`) **includes amendments that are promulgated but not in effect yet**, every article labeled with the current effective date: the Labor Standards Act shows 제44조의4 "in effect since 2026-10-08", but it takes effect on 2027-01-01, and 제13조 and 제101조 already show text from 2026-12-08. | The text is always read by effective date (`target=eflaw`) for the version in force on `as_of` (default today); later amendments are listed in `upcoming`. An article that is not in force yet is returned marked `not_yet_in_force`, with the date it takes effect. | ✅ |
| law.go.kr | Searching `민법` returns 난민법, 난민법 시행규칙 and 난민법 시행령 **before 민법**: results are sorted by name only. | Exact matches first, then prefixes, then acts before decrees and ordinances. | ✅ |
| law.go.kr (precedents) | A case number with spaces (`2020 다 296604`) or a date range as `2026-01-01~2026-12-31` is **success with zero results**. | Spaces are removed; dates are taken as `YYYY-MM-DD` and validated. | ✅ |
| ECOS | Asking a table for its first item only returns **every combination of the other item dimensions under the same dates**: monthly KRW/USD (`731Y004`) gives January 2025 as both 1,455.79 (monthly average) and 1,433.3 (month-end). | Rows are grouped into one series per item combination, each labeled; `usd_krw` asks for the average explicitly. | ✅ |
| ECOS | A period's value means different things per table: the monthly, quarterly and annual **base rate is the end-of-period level** (2024 annual: 3.00%, though it was 3.50% for most of the year), market rates are averages of daily values, and annual current account is a sum. | Every series has `aggregation`: period average, end of period or period total. | ✅ |
| ECOS (employment) | The unemployment rate table returns **two series per month unless asked for one**: September 2026 is 2.0% (not seasonally adjusted, the headline figure) and 2.6% (seasonally adjusted). | Both are returned, labeled 원계열 and 계절조정, with a note saying which is the headline. | ✅ |
| ECOS | CPI and house prices are **index levels** (2020=100, KB 2026.01=100), not inflation rates; a year-on-year change computed on a rate (unemployment 3.8% → 4.0%) as a percent gives 5.26% instead of 0.2 points. | Index indicators add `yoy_percent`; for rates the change is `yoy_points`. | ✅ |

## Errors that look like something else

| Source | What the API does | What korea-data-mcp does | |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenDART | Errors (bad key, quota exceeded, no data) come back as **HTTP 200** with a `status` field. | Mapped to standard error codes, with the original in `upstream_code`. Error responses are never cached. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr | An unregistered key is **HTTP 403**, which looks like a permission problem. | `UNAUTHENTICATED` with a hint: apply for the API, use the Decoding key, wait for activation. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr | The same error document (`OpenAPI_ServiceResponse`) can also arrive with **HTTP 200**, e.g. for a malformed `numOfRows`. | Detected in any status, in XML or JSON. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr | Success is `resultCode` `00` in some services and `000` in others; "no data" is `03` or `003`. | All normalized: success and "no data" both return a list (possibly empty). | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (business status) | "Not registered" is not an error or a status: the message `국세청에 등록되지 않은 사업자등록번호입니다.` is put in the **tax type** field. | `registered: false`, with no tax type. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr (business status) | The National Tax Service API has its own error format and unusual HTTP statuses: an empty request is 411 (Length Required), over 100 numbers is 413, a bad key is 401 with `{"code":-4,...}`. The gateway also returns an occasional 504. | Mapped to the standard errors (the key error gets the same hint as the other APIs); 504 is retried. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr | A newly approved key returns `SERVICE_KEY_IS_NOT_REGISTERED_ERROR` for up to a few hours. | The hint says so, so the agent tells the user to wait instead of guessing. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr | A superseded endpoint (the stock price API before `_V2`) also answers **"key not registered" (30)**, not "no such service" (12), so an approved key looks broken. | Every tool calls the endpoint shown on the API's page today. | ✅ |
| law.go.kr | Errors are **HTTP 200** documents of different shapes: `<Response>` for a bad OC, `<Law>` with plain text for an unknown law. | `UNAUTHENTICATED` with a hint on where to find the OC; an unknown law is `NOT_FOUND`. | ✅ |
| law.go.kr | Asking for a law version by a date (`efYd`) that is not exactly one of its effective dates returns an **empty document** `{}`, and a missing article returns the law without articles. | The version is chosen from the law's history, and a missing article is `NOT_FOUND` with a hint when an upcoming amendment may add it. | ✅ |
| ECOS | Errors are **HTTP 200** with `{"RESULT":{"CODE":"INFO-200"}}`, and an unknown table code gives the same "no data" as a period with no data. | `NOT_FOUND`, and an unknown code is checked against the table list and reported as `unknown stat_code`. A bad key is `UNAUTHENTICATED`. | ✅ |
| ECOS | The API key is **part of the URL path** (`/api/StatisticSearch/<key>/json/…`), not the query string, so the usual query stripping does not hide it. | The key is masked in every error message. | ✅ |

## Formats that differ per API

| Source | What the API does | What korea-data-mcp does | |
|---|---|---|---|
| data.go.kr | In JSON, one result is an **object**, several are an **array**, and none is `""` in one service and `{"item":[]}` in another. Numbers are strings in some fields and numbers in others (`"locdate": 20261225`). | korea-data-mcp requests XML, which has none of these problems, and outputs typed JSON. | ✅ |
| data.go.kr | Some services are XML only (apartment trades), others JSON or XML, and the National Tax Service API is on a different host (`api.odcloud.kr`) with POST requests. | One provider, one output format. | ✅ |
| All | Dates come as `20251114`, `2024.12.31 현재`, `25.11.28`, `75/06/11`. | ISO 8601 (`2025-11-14`). | ✅ |
| All | Amounts come as `"1,234"`, `"-"` (no value), or `"(1,234)"` (negative). | Integers, or `null` when there is no value (never a misleading `0`). | ✅ |
| data.go.kr | Empty fields are `""` or a single space `" "`, and addresses have double spaces. Some dates are not dates at all (a founding date of `99`). | Whitespace is cleaned; empty and invalid values are omitted. | ✅ |
| All | Pagination is `pageNo`/`numOfRows`, `page_no`/`page_count`, or none. | Always `page`/`page_size` in, `page_info` out. | ✅ |
| law.go.kr (precedents) | National Tax Service records have no court name; the court is inside the case number (`서울행정법원-2025-구합-53785`). Text uses `<br/>`, some fields are the string `"null"`, and dates are `2025.10.16` in search and `20251016` in details. | `court` is filled from the case number, which becomes `2025구합53785`; text has plain line breaks; dates are ISO. | ✅ |
| ECOS | Units are only in `UNIT_NAME` and differ per table (`십억원`, `백만달러`, `억원`, `천명`); yen is per 100; periods are `20251001`, `202510`, `2025Q3`, `2025S1` or `2025`. | Amounts become KRW or USD (`source_unit` keeps the original), labels say "per 100 yen", and periods are `2025-10-01`, `2025-10`, `2025-Q3`, `2025-H1`, `2025`. | ✅ |
| ECOS | The daily and the monthly table of the same market rate use **different item codes** (3-year treasury: `010200000` daily, `5020000` monthly). | Named indicators pick the right table and code for each frequency. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (major decisions) | Each kind of decision has its own field names (`cv_prc`, `ex_prc`; `bddd`, `aq_dd`, `dp_dd`), dates are text (`2026년 01월 30일`), rates are `100` or `100.0`, and rows are not sorted. | One output shape per kind, ISO dates, numbers, latest filing first. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (investments) | The last row is a `합계` (total), preferred shares are a separate row (`㈜스파크플러스(우선주)`), and names carry layout whitespace and notes (` 에스케이하이닉스㈜ `, `NITTAN VALVE\n(해외법인\n일본상장)`). | Totals are computed from the stakes; `preferred: true`; names are cleaned and notes moved to `name_note`. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (investments) | The purpose is free text in over 40 forms: `경영참여`, `경영참가`, `경영\n참여`, `경영권참여`, `단순투자`, `일반투자`, `투자`, or a sentence about palm oil. | `purpose` is `control`, `investment` or `other`, with the original in `purpose_ko`, and can be filtered. | ✅ |
| OpenDART (full statements) | Rows are **ordered by account ID, not as printed**: Samsung's income statement starts with 영업이익, 기타수익, 판매비와관리비 and ends with 매출액, and there is no parent or level field. Periods are labels (`제 58 기 반기`) without dates. | Main lines in printed order (매출액, 매출원가, 매출총이익, 판관비, 영업이익, ...), others after; dates computed for every amount. | ✅ |

## Checked, and fine

Things we suspected, tested on the live APIs, and found to behave correctly.

- **OpenDART corrections (기재정정).** A periodic report can be corrected several times, months or years later. For 43 corrected reports of listed companies filed in September and October 2025, the financials, dividends and major shareholder APIs returned the latest correction every time. `receipt_no` identifies the filing the values come from.
- **OpenDART corrections of major decisions.** The decision APIs also return only the latest correction of each decision (Samsung's three filings of its November 2024 buyback come back as one), but see the date range above. The 5% holdings API does the same: Samsung C&T's report of 2025-01-17, corrected on 2025-01-24, comes back once, as the correction.
